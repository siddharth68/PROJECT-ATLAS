Formal Clinical Definition of Hy's Law

                        

                            1. Aminotransferase Elevation: ALT or AST > 3.0 × Upper Limit of Normal (ULN).

                            2. Bilirubin Elevation: Total Bilirubin > 2.0 × ULN within 14 calendar days of the qualifying transaminase elevation.

                            3. Exclusions: No pre-existing baseline chronic liver disease or alternative medical explanation.
